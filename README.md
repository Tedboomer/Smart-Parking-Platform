# Smart-Parking-Platform
Project for CIS4374

## Project Kickoff

### Company Introduction

Ted Corp. is a fictional software development company that creates technology solutions for businesses and the public. The company has been selected to plan and design a Smart Parking Platform that will make parking easier for drivers and easier to manage for parking operators.

For this project, the company has access to unlimited theoretical resources and budget. However, the project must still be completed within the semester timeline. The development team will include people working on the mobile application, web application, backend systems, maps, payments, and software testing.

## How the Project Was Acquired

Ted Corp. was contacted by parking facility operators and city transportation representatives who wanted a better way to manage parking. Their current systems make it difficult for drivers to quickly find open parking spaces, especially in busy areas.

After reviewing the problem, Ted Corp. proposed the Smart Parking Platform. The project was approved because it could help drivers save time while also helping parking operators better manage their available spaces.

## Project Overview

The Smart Parking Platform will be a web and mobile system that helps drivers locate, reserve, and pay for parking. Users will be able to search nearby parking locations, view available spaces, compare parking options, reserve a space, and make a digital payment.

The platform will also provide tools for parking facility operators. Operators will be able to monitor parking occupancy, manage parking locations, review payments, view reports, and receive information about how their parking spaces are being used.

The system will also connect to outside services such as mapping applications and payment providers. This will allow users to receive directions to their parking location and make secure electronic payments.

## Business Need

Drivers often spend unnecessary time searching for parking in crowded areas. This can cause traffic congestion, wasted fuel, frustration, and delays. Drivers also may not know the price of parking or whether spaces are available until they reach the location.

Parking facility operators also need better ways to monitor available spaces and understand how their facilities are being used. A Smart Parking Platform can help both groups by putting parking information, reservations, payments, and management tools into one system.

The main business need is to create a system that makes parking easier for customers while helping parking operators improve the use of their parking facilities.

## Project Vision

The vision of the Smart Parking Platform is to create one easy-to-use system that allows drivers to find and manage parking from their phone or computer.

The platform should reduce the amount of time drivers spend searching for parking. It should also provide accurate parking information and make reservations and payments simple.

For parking operators, the platform should provide useful information about parking demand, availability, payments, and occupancy. The goal is to create a reliable system that benefits both drivers and parking businesses.

## Project Scope

The Smart Parking Platform will include a mobile application and web application. Drivers will be able to create accounts, sign in, search for parking, view parking locations on a map, reserve parking, pay electronically, and review previous reservations.

Parking operators will have access to an administrative dashboard. This dashboard will allow them to monitor parking locations, view occupancy information, manage parking data, and review reports.

The project will also include connections to third-party mapping and payment services. Security and privacy requirements will be considered because the system will store account, vehicle, reservation, and payment information.

The project will focus on the planning and design of the complete software system during the semester.

## Competitive Analysis

### SpotHero

SpotHero allows drivers to search for parking, compare prices, reserve parking spaces, and pay before arriving.

One strength of SpotHero is that it makes parking reservations simple and allows users to compare different parking options.

One weakness is that parking availability depends on participating garages and parking facilities.

### ParkMobile

ParkMobile allows users to find and pay for parking using a mobile application. Users can select a parking zone, choose how long they want to park, and pay through the app.

One strength of ParkMobile is that it makes parking payments quick and easy.

One weakness is that the parking process may be different depending on the city or parking location.

### ParkWhiz

ParkWhiz allows users to search for parking by address, location, or event. Users can compare parking locations, reserve parking, and pay before arriving.

One strength of ParkWhiz is its simple reservation process.

One weakness is that different parking facilities may use different methods to verify reservations.

## How Our Platform Will Be Different

The Smart Parking Platform will combine useful features from existing parking applications while focusing on both drivers and parking operators.

The platform will include real-time parking availability, navigation support, operator dashboards, parking analytics, notifications, occupancy reporting, reservations, and payments in one system.

Parking operators will also have better tools to monitor how their parking facilities are being used.

The goal is to create a consistent experience where drivers can search, reserve, pay, navigate, and receive parking information using one platform.

# Homework 2 - Work Breakdown Structure and Project Timeline

## Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

The Work Breakdown Structure breaks the Smart Parking Platform into smaller parts. This makes the project easier to organize and helps the development teams understand what work needs to be completed.

## 1.0 Smart Parking Platform

### 1.1 Authentication System

#### 1.1.1 User Registration
- Allow new users to create an account
- Collect name, email, password, and basic information
- Check that required information is entered

#### 1.1.2 User Login
- Allow registered users to log in
- Verify email and password
- Display an error if the login information is incorrect

#### 1.1.3 Session Management
- Keep users logged in while using the system
- Allow users to log out
- Protect user accounts from unauthorized access

### 1.2 User and Operator Management

#### 1.2.1 User Profile
- Allow users to view their profile
- Allow users to update personal information
- Allow users to change their password

#### 1.2.2 Vehicle Management
- Allow users to add a vehicle
- Store license plate information
- Allow users to edit or remove a vehicle

#### 1.2.3 Parking Operator Setup
- Allow parking operators to create an operator account
- Allow operators to manage their information
- Give operators access to parking management tools

### 1.3 Parking Dashboard

#### 1.3.1 Garage Management
- Add new parking garages
- Edit garage information
- Remove parking garages
- Update parking prices
- Update hours of operation

#### 1.3.2 Find Parking
- Search for nearby parking
- View parking locations on a map
- Show available parking spaces
- Show parking prices

#### 1.3.3 Parking Reservation
- Select a parking location
- Choose a parking date and time
- Reserve a parking space
- Cancel a reservation if needed

### 1.4 Payment System

#### 1.4.1 Payment Information
- Allow users to enter payment information
- Connect the system to a third-party payment provider
- Protect payment information

#### 1.4.2 Process Payment
- Allow users to pay for parking
- Confirm successful payments
- Display an error if a payment fails

#### 1.4.3 Receipts
- Create a receipt after payment
- Allow users to view past receipts
- Store payment history

### 1.5 Garage Monitoring

#### 1.5.1 Parking Availability
- Monitor available parking spaces
- Update parking availability
- Show spaces as available or occupied

#### 1.5.2 Occupancy Monitoring
- Track how full each parking garage is
- Display occupancy information to operators
- Update occupancy information when cars enter or leave

#### 1.5.3 Notifications
- Notify users about reservations
- Notify users when parking time is almost finished
- Send alerts about parking changes

### 1.6 Reporting System

#### 1.6.1 Occupancy Reports
- Show garage occupancy information
- Create graphs showing parking usage
- Compare parking activity over time

#### 1.6.2 Financial Reports
- Show parking payments
- Show total revenue
- Allow reports to be exported

#### 1.6.3 Usage Reports
- Show the number of reservations
- Show popular parking locations
- Help operators understand parking activity

### 1.7 Testing

#### 1.7.1 Authentication Testing
- Test registration
- Test login
- Test logout

#### 1.7.2 Parking System Testing
- Test parking search
- Test reservations
- Test garage availability

#### 1.7.3 Payment Testing
- Test successful payments
- Test failed payments
- Test receipts

#### 1.7.4 Final System Testing
- Test the complete system
- Fix major problems
- Make sure all main requirements work
